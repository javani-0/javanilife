# Javani Student Portal — Update on your six points

**Date:** 10 August 2026

Thank you for the detailed list. We checked every point against your **live data**
before changing anything, so what follows is what was actually happening — not a
guess. All six are now addressed and live.

One finding is worth stating up front, because it explains most of the list:

> **The Attendance, Assignments, Exams and Certificates screens were already built
> and working — but nobody could reach them.** There was no teacher login on the
> system at all, and the one manager account did not have those two screens
> switched on. So the screens sat empty because nothing had ever been entered,
> not because they were broken. We have now given teachers a proper way in.

---

## 1. Attendance tracking & teacher access

**You said:** teachers have no clear place to mark attendance; is there a separate
teacher login?

**What we found:** there was no teacher login. Attendance could only be reached by
a full administrator, and no attendance had ever been recorded.

**What we did:** created a proper **Teacher login**.

- Create one under **Managers → Teachers**: name, email, password, and tick the
  classes that teacher handles.
- **Share login** sends their details straight to them on WhatsApp.
- A teacher signs in and sees **only Attendance and Academics**, and **only their
  own classes**. They cannot open fees, student records or finance.
- Marking attendance is: pick the class and date, everyone starts as Present, tap
  anyone who is absent, Save. Re-opening the same date lets you edit it.

Attendance flows straight through to the student's dashboard, and their attendance
percentage feeds into their progress report.

---

## 2. Assignments

**You said:** assignments added under Classes Manager are not showing for students.

**What we found:** assignments were created on a separate Academics page rather
than inside Classes Manager, and none had ever been created — the collection was
completely empty. The student side was wired up correctly and was waiting for
content.

**What we did:** you can now set assignments **from inside the class itself**.

- **Classes Manager → open a class → Academics → Assignments**
- Give it a title, instructions, a due date and optional marks
- Students see it immediately on their **Assignments** page, upload their work as a
  PDF, and you read it, mark it and leave feedback in the same place

---

## 3. Exam schedules

**You said:** the Exams section on the student dashboard is empty.

**What we found:** the same cause — the tool existed, no exam had ever been added.

**What we did:** exams are now scheduled from **Classes Manager → the class →
Academics → Exams**. Add the date, time, venue and syllabus and it appears under
**Upcoming** on the student's Exams page. When you release hall tickets, each
eligible student gets a printable hall ticket with a QR code.

---

## 4. Certificates

**You said:** where should teachers issue certificates?

**Answer:** **Classes Manager → the class → Academics → Certificates.**

Pick the student, give the certificate a title and date, upload the certificate
image, and issue it. It appears on that student's **Certificates** page. You can
revoke one later if it was issued in error.

---

## 5. EMI payments & enrolled courses

**You said:** the EMI Payments tab shows nothing, while the Classes tab shows EMI
details but not which course the student is enrolled in.

**What we found:** this one was a genuine fault on our side, and you had read it
exactly right. The EMI Payments page was only looking at instalments from the
**online shop**, and never at **class fee instalments** — which is where all of
your EMI students actually are. Six students are on class EMI plans, and their
instalments were being shown on the Classes tab instead.

**What we did:**

- **EMI Payments** now shows **class fee instalments** first — the course name,
  how much is paid, how much is left, and a Pay button on each pending instalment.
  Shop instalments still appear underneath for anyone who has them.
- **My Classes** now leads with the **course name** as the heading, with the
  **class timing** and the **trainer** beneath it, and only a one-line EMI summary
  that links across to the EMI tab. Payments happen in one place now, not two.

We also fixed a related problem while we were in there: where a student's saved
batch had been removed from the class, **no class timing was showing at all**.
Timings now fall back to the class's own weekly schedule, so a time is always
shown.

## 6. Class materials, joining links & recordings

**You said:** PDFs, live links and recordings added in Class Management are not
visible to students.

**What we found:** two separate causes, and neither was the upload itself.

1. **Most classes have no content uploaded.** Of your five active classes, only two
   have anything at all — a live link and one PDF between them. The other three
   (**KP Grades System**, **NATTUVANGAM GRADES**, **DIPLOMA IN KUCHIPUDI 2nd SEM**)
   have no live link, no recordings and no materials, so there is genuinely nothing
   for those students to open. KP Grades System alone has four students waiting.
2. **Three students are attached to a class that was deleted.** Anagha, Prerana and
   Karuna are linked to classes that no longer exist on the system, so they could
   never see content no matter what was uploaded.

**What we did:**

- Materials, recordings and the live link now appear **directly on the class card**
  in the student's My Classes — previously they were only visible after clicking
  into the class.
- Classes Manager now **warns you on any class that has nothing uploaded**, so an
  empty class is obvious at a glance instead of looking finished.
- Student Manager now shows a **banner listing any student attached to a deleted
  class**, with a dropdown to re-link them to the correct class. Please do this for
  the three students named above — it takes a few seconds each and restores their
  access immediately.
